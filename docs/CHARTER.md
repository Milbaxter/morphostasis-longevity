# Research charter

Long-term objective: contribute testable knowledge toward rejuvenation and longer healthy life by investigating cellular coordination, bioelectric organization, and evolutionary gene-expression patterns.

The initial release tests three questions: whether spatial organization in a single-donor keratinocyte culture model is distinguishable from imaging and density effects; whether predefined electrical-signaling machinery changes expression across human epithelial aging/senescence cohorts; and whether evolutionary gene-age associations survive detection/expression/length controls.

Evidence classes: published observation, replicated result, exploratory extension, computational control, and unmeasured mechanistic prediction. No transcriptomic measurement is labeled membrane voltage, coupling, organismal rejuvenation, or lifespan extension. Culture duration and donor age are distinct exposures. Negative and unresolved results are first-class outcomes.

## Prospective specification rule

Each workstream's specification and fixed panels must be committed before its new hypothesis tests run. Dataset metadata and file headers may be inspected beforehand. Record deviations and their timestamps; these specifications are not external registered reports and existing published outcomes were known during planning.

## Roles and integration

GPT-6 Luna, high reasoning: image/spatial analysis; epithelial expression; AGED reproduction/extension. The coordinating agent maintains provenance, integration, and reporting. A separate clean-environment review must inspect reported results. Agents must distinguish implemented tests from proposed tests and successful downloads from advertised availability.

## Resource and data policy

Local compute only, no purchases. Cache at most 10 GiB, keep at least 10 GiB free disk, and use bounded requests. Do not download the full approximately 20 GB image archive or planarian FASTQs. Inspect archive metadata and use validated byte ranges. Missing labels or images must not be manufactured. Biological units are donors or independent cultures; cells, ROIs, images, and technical resamples remain nested observations.

Original code: MIT. Original documentation: CC BY 4.0. External data/code retain their original terms. Source URLs and checksums are published; third-party raw data are downloaded locally rather than committed by default.

## Completion

Release 0.1 requires actual results for accessible analyses, explicit data gates for blocked analyses, reproducibility commands, statistical/provenance checks, an evidence-based next-experiment handoff, and a decision for each hypothesis. Further work prioritizes the uncertainty most likely to change a biological decision.
