# Evidence register

The aim is to identify discriminating tests. Published findings, our reanalyses,
and proposed mechanisms are separate evidence classes. This register was compiled
before the new analyses; links lead to original papers/data rather than summaries.

## Important unmeasured links

1. Whether a spatial electrical organization change causes senescence, rather than
   reporting density, metabolism, dye behavior, or cellular state.
2. Whether restoring spatial organization at comparable mean voltage improves a
   functional endpoint while retaining cell identity.
3. Whether gene-expression changes correspond to altered channel activity or
   functional electrical coupling.
4. Whether evolutionary gene-age enrichment measures a biological loss of
   coordination, rather than properties of gene measurement and selection.
5. Whether any candidate mechanism improves organismal healthspan or lifespan.

The first release can constrain measurements and prioritize these links. It cannot
establish unmeasured links through narrative synthesis.
